The plaintiffs challenged the validity of a litigation funding agreement and a subsequent Distribution Agreement that divided proceeds from settlements with PVL's former directors and auditors. The High Court dismissed all plaintiffs' claims, finding the former liquidator did not breach his duties or act under undue influence when making commercial compromises with the funders.
