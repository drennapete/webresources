    "summary": "Mainzeal operated as a major construction company while balance sheet insolvent for years, relying on unenforceable assurances of group support. After it collapsed into liquidation, liquidators sued the directors for breaches of s 135 (reckless trading) and s 136 (duty in relation to obligations) of the Companies Act 1993. The Supreme Court held that the directors breached s 135 by continuing to trade in a manner likely to create a substantial risk of serious loss to creditors, and breached s 136 by agreeing to incur new obligations without reasonable grounds to believe they would be met. The Court held that the appropriate measure of compensation for s 136 is the 'new debt' approach. Using its discretion under s 301, the Court ordered $39.8m in compensation, holding Mr Yan fully liable for this amount, while capping the liability of the other directors at $6.6m each due to their lesser culpability.",
